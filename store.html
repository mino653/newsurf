Getting Started

Quick guide to start playing and understanding the basics

Start Playing

When you load the page, click the "Back to Game" button or click anywhere on the screen. Your mouse cursor will disappear and lock into the game, giving you full first-person control.

  • Pause / Unlock Mouse: Press the Escape (Esc) key at any time to open the menu, unlock your cursor, adjust settings, or take a break.

Movement & Looking Around

  • Mouse: Move your mouse around to look in any direction (up at the sky, down at the ground, or all around you).
  • W: Walk forward.
  • S: Walk backward.
  • A: Strafe (step) left.
  • D: Strafe (step) right.
  • Spacebar: Jump onto blocks or over obstacles.

Building & Mining Blocks

  • Crosshair: Aim the white plus icon (+) in the center of your screen directly at the block face you want to interact with.
  • Left Click (Mine / Break): Destroys the block you are pointing at immediately.
  • Right Click (Place): Places your currently selected block onto whatever surface you are pointing at.

Selecting Blocks (Hotbar)

Press the number keys 1 through 6 on your keyboard to switch what material you are holding:

  • Key 1: Grass Block (Vibrant green top, dirt sides)
  • Key 2: Dirt Block (Rich brown)
  • Key 3: Stone Block (Solid gray)
  • Key 4: Oak Wood Log (Tree trunk bark)
  • Key 5: Oak Leaves (Green foliage)
  • Key 6: Wooden Planks (Light building wood)

Health, Hunger & Hazards

  • Red Hearts (Health Bar): You start with 10 full hearts.
  • The Void: If you walk off the edge of the floating island and fall down into the deep blue sky, you will start taking void damage (losing 1 heart every second).
  • Dying & Respawning: If all 10 hearts run out, your health resets to full, and you automatically respawn right back in the center of the island.
  • Manual Respawn: If you ever get stuck or fall, press Esc to open the menu and click the gray "Respawn" button to teleport back onto solid ground.

Game Settings

  • Field of View (FOV): Press Esc to bring up the menu, then drag the FOV Slider to adjust how wide your camera view is (from standard 75° to wide-angle 110°).
